Essential Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit

The best seasons to visit Nepal are Spring (March-May) and Autumn (September-November) with clear skies and pleasant temperatures.

Visa Information

Most visitors can obtain a visa on arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport. Tourist visas are available for 15, 30, or 90 days.

Currency

The Nepalese Rupee (NPR) is the local currency. ATMs are available in major cities, but carry some cash for remote areas.

Language

Nepali is the official language, but English is widely spoken in tourist areas and establishments.

Electricity

Nepal uses 230V AC electricity with type C, D, and M power plugs. Bring a universal adapter for your devices.

Health & Safety

Consult your doctor about vaccinations before travel. Purchase travel insurance with adequate coverage for trekking activities.

About Nepal Tourism

Nepal, nestled in the heart of the Himalayas, is a land of breathtaking natural beauty, rich cultural heritage, and warm hospitality. Home to eight of the world's fourteen highest peaks, including Mount Everest, it offers some of the most spectacular mountain scenery and trekking opportunities on earth.

Beyond its majestic mountains, Nepal boasts diverse landscapes ranging from subtropical forests to alpine meadows. The country's cultural heritage is equally impressive, with ancient temples, historic cities, and vibrant festivals reflecting its Hindu and Buddhist traditions.

From the bustling streets of Kathmandu to the serene lakes of Pokhara, from the jungle wildlife of Chitwan to the sacred birthplace of Buddha in Lumbini, Nepal offers unforgettable experiences for every type of traveler.
